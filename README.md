## 🌟 About The App

**FilmiYaar** is a hyper-personalized movie discovery engine. Built around a sleek, Tinder-style swipeable interface, it escapes the traditional "genre traps" of standard movie databases by building a multi-dimensional **Taste DNA** profile for every user. 

By analyzing implicit behavioral feedback (trailer clicks, saving, loving, skipping), FilmiYaar natively understands the deep themes, keywords, and specific actors/directors you are subconsciously drawn to, creating a truly personal recommendation layer on top of standard TMDB data.

### ✨ Key Features

- **🔥 "Your Kind of Movie":** The flagship recommendation engine that aggressively filters candidates through your unique Taste DNA, ensuring you only see titles highly likely to resonate with you.
- **🆕 New Releases For You:** A rolling 90-day filter that maps brand new box office releases against your personal taste vector.
- **🧬 Taste Passport Visualizer:** A beautiful profile dashboard that transparently shows you the *exact* deep themes, genres, and creators the algorithm has learned you love.
- **🎬 Actionable Discovery:** Full integration with JustWatch watch-providers to instantly tell you exactly *where* to stream a recommended movie in your specific region.
